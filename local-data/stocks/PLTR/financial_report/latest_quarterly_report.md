## Executive Summary

Palantir delivered its highest-growth quarter since 2021, with total revenue of $828M (+36% YoY), powered by explosive U.S. Commercial AIP adoption and record U.S. Government contract activity. GAAP operating income reached $134M (16.2% margin), the seventh consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability. EPS of $0.14 (GAAP) beat consensus of $0.11.

## Key Risks

1. **Valuation** — Trades at ~120x forward earnings and ~40x forward revenue; priced for perfection. Any growth miss triggers severe multiple compression.
2. **Government budget risk** — DOGE-driven discretionary cuts could delay or reduce DoD/civilian agency contracts despite bipartisan AI support.
3. **Commercial concentration** — Top 20 U.S. commercial customers represent ~55% of segment revenue; churn risk elevated.
4. **International stagnation** — International commercial growth (22%) significantly lags U.S. (63%); European data sovereignty concerns limit AIP deployments.
5. **Competition** — AWS, Azure, and Google offering embedded AI platforms at lower incremental cost to existing cloud customers; commoditization risk for mid-market.
6. **SBC dilution** — Stock-based compensation of $265M in FY2025 creates ongoing dilution (~1.5% annually).
