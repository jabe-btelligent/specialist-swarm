## Segment Deep-Dives

### iPhone
iPhone 17 Pro Max remained supply-constrained through November. U.S. upgrades accelerated as Apple Intelligence features (on-device LLM, enhanced Siri) drove switcher rates to a 5-year high. ASP rose to ~$910 (+3% YoY).

### Services
Services gross margin expanded to 75.4% (vs 73.9% prior year). Advertising revenue within the App Store grew 18% YoY. Apple Intelligence Pro tier launched in January 2026, contributing to early subscriber growth.

### Greater China
Revenue of $23.4B (+6% YoY) reversed three quarters of declines. Demand for locally manufactured iPhone 17 units and renewed carrier promotions drove the recovery.

## Key Risks

1. **China regulatory environment** — ongoing scrutiny of App Store practices by Chinese authorities.
2. **Antitrust (EU & US)** — Digital Markets Act compliance costs; potential loss of Google default search deal (~$18B annual TAC payment from Alphabet).
3. **Supply chain concentration** — 90%+ of iPhone production remains in China despite ongoing India ramp.
4. **AI monetization** — Apple Intelligence adoption rate and willingness-to-pay for premium tiers remains unproven at scale.
5. **Valuation** — Trades at ~28x forward P/E vs. 5-year average of 22x; premium requires sustained Services re-rating.
