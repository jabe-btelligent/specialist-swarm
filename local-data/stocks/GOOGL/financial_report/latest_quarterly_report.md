## Executive Summary

Alphabet posted a strong Q4 2025, with total revenues of $100.2B (+14% YoY) as Search resilience defied AI-disruption fears, YouTube continued to gain streaming share, and Google Cloud crossed a $50B annualized run-rate for the first time. EPS of $2.39 beat consensus of $2.27. The board authorized a $70B incremental buyback program.

## Google Cloud Deep-Dive

Google Cloud revenue of $12.3B grew 28% YoY (excluding the settlement). Cloud operating income reached $1.5B (12.2% margin), a new record, up from $900M in Q4 2024.

- **Vertex AI** platform: 5,000+ enterprise customers running production workloads
- **Gemini Pro 2.0** API calls grew 6x QoQ following the December launch
- Signed 15 multi-year contracts >$500M in Q4 alone
- TPU v5e and v6 deployment enabling cost-competitive AI inference vs. NVIDIA

---

## Search & AI Overviews

AI Overviews (formerly SGE) now deployed to 3.5 billion monthly users in 140+ countries. Click-through rate impact:

- Overall search click-through rates remained stable; monetization gap vs. traditional 10-blue-links closing
- Search revenue grew 12.5% despite AI mode rollout — stronger than feared
- Advertising CPCs up 4% YoY; impression volume up 8%

## Key Risks

1. **Search disruption** — AI-native search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search) gaining traction among under-35 demographic; long-term query volume risk.
2. **DOJ antitrust** — Remedies in U.S. v. Google could include Chrome/Android divestiture, end of default search agreements (loss of ~$18B from Apple alone).
3. **Regulatory (EU)** — DMA compliance forcing search and shopping changes; ad revenue impact uncertain.
4. **YouTube competition** — TikTok ban/sale uncertainty; if TikTok persists, ad share competition intensifies.
5. **Capex escalation** — $75B FY2026 guidance compresses FCF; ROI on AI infrastructure spend not yet visible in financials.
